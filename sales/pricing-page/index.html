  • The agent runs in your own Microsoft tenant, with your Dynamics 365 security roles.
  • It reads and writes your data only there. Our licence service never receives your business data or personal data.

Questions

Frequently asked

Why are Microsoft costs separate?

They're for Microsoft's service, billed on your own Microsoft agreement. Keeping them separate means you see exactly what Microsoft charges, you control the limit, and we don't add a margin on top.

What happens if we go over the work items in our plan?

The agent keeps working. Extra items are charged at your plan's rate. If you're regularly over, we'll suggest the plan that's cheaper for you.

What happens when the subscription ends?

You get reminders in the agent's reports from 30 days before, and a 7-day grace period. After that the agent stops starting new work and hands open items to your people. Work already under way finishes, and your audit log and data stay in your environment.

What if your service is unavailable?

The agent keeps working for several hours on the rules it already has, and its reports say so. Only a longer outage pauses new work, which then goes to your people.

Do our users need Microsoft 365 Copilot licences?

No. Users who already have them can chat with the agent at no Copilot credit cost. Work the agent does on its own is charged as Copilot credits either way.

Can we change the rules the agent follows?

Yes. Thresholds such as amounts and days are settings in your environment that your administrator can change. Changes to the rules themselves are part of your plan's included changes.
