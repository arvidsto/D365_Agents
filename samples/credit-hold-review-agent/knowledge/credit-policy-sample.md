# Contoso Credit Policy (sample — for the Credit Hold Review Agent knowledge source)

*Fictitious policy for demonstration. Replace with your company's approved credit policy.*

## 1. Purpose
Protect Contoso from bad debt while avoiding unnecessary delays for customers who pay reliably.

## 2. Who decides
- Orders up to 100,000 (company currency): Credit Manager.
- Orders above 100,000: Credit Manager **and** Finance Controller.
- Intercompany orders: excluded from credit holds; route any exception to the Finance Controller.

## 3. Release guidelines
A held order can normally be released when:
- the oldest overdue invoice is no more than 15 days late,
- total exposure (balance + new order) stays within 110% of the credit limit,
- there is no open dispute or complaint with the customer, and
- the customer has made a payment in the last 90 days.

## 4. Keep-on-hold guidelines
Keep the order on hold when:
- any invoice is more than 60 days overdue,
- total exposure exceeds 150% of the credit limit,
- the credit limit has expired without a scheduled review, or
- a person placed a forced credit hold.

## 5. Strategic customers
A large sales pipeline is a reason to **escalate quickly**, not a reason to release automatically.

## 6. Documentation
Every release or rejection must record the reason and the approver. Decisions are reviewed monthly.
